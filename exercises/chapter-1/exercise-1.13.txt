Exercise 1.13: Prove that {\text{Fib}(n)} is the closest integer
to {\varphi^n / \sqrt{5}}, where \varphi = {(1 + \sqrt{5}) / 2}.
Hint: Let \psi = {(1 - \sqrt{5}) / 2}.  Use induction and the
definition of the Fibonacci numbers (see 1.2.2) to prove
that {\text{Fib}(n)} = {(\varphi^n - \psi^n) / \sqrt{5}}.
